<br>

I'm **Ansh Kalra**, building under the name **DRAG**. I sit at the seam between three things most people keep separate: **security**, **systems**, and **design**. I like taking something intimidating — an auth server, a malware sample, a broken PDF, a raw video — and turning it into something a normal person can actually use.

If it can be self-hosted, reverse-engineered, or made beautiful, it has my attention.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b><code>cat security.md</code></b></summary>

<br>

The part of the craft I keep coming back to:

- **Reverse engineering & malware analysis** — pulling apart binaries and samples to understand how they really work.
- **Offensive tooling & red-team craft** — building and studying the tools attackers use, so the defensive side is grounded in reality.
- **Authentication & licensing systems** — signing, device binding, session enforcement, and the failure modes that actually get exploited.
- **Web & application security** — finding the logic flaws, not just the obvious ones.
- **CTF** — because there's no faster way to learn than a scoreboard.

I build security *into* things from the first commit, rather than bolting it on later.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b><code>ls ~/builds</code></b></summary>

<br>

Real, shipped software — not slideware:

- **[Keyforge](https://github.com/ansh2807/keyforge)** — self-hosted software licensing & authentication (Ed25519-signed, Argon2id, SDKs for 5 languages).
- **[PDF Studio](https://github.com/ansh2807/pdf-studio)** — a full browser PDF editor with a self-hosted engine; nothing leaves your server.
- **[Scribe Studio](https://github.com/ansh2807/scribe-studio)** — local transcription with Indic-language routing, plus a video editor & target-size compressor.
- **[Omniscope](https://github.com/ansh2807/omniscope)** — creator & web intelligence that shows its formulas and fabricates nothing.

The through-line: **you own the keys, the data, and the machine.**

</details>

## ▸ How I build

**Security in the design.** &nbsp;Authentication, permissions, and data boundaries get attention from the first commit — not the last.

**Control over the stack.** &nbsp;I gravitate toward software people can run on their own infrastructure, with clear choices about every external service.

**Craft at the surface.** &nbsp;Typography, motion, and interaction should make a tool easier to understand and better to use.
